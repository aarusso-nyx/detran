# ADR-0055: Executable invariant producers and additive historical compatibility

## Status

Accepted by Architect on2026-10-19 under the Owner-authorized C-0004 scope.

## Context

The Owner authorized C5–C8 through campaign C-0004. R-0041 owns new threat/error references, executable invariant bindings, CI convergence and historical compatibility. This supplements ADR-0053 without rewriting its decision.

## Decision

Bind seven invariant obligations to actual scoped test populations and exact executed test IDs through the existing adopter-policy and official init binding. A generic successful test command does not discharge an unrelated semantic invariant. Native service or runner limitations remain blockers; no synthetic native receipts or test IDs are admitted. Preserve the constitution version/pin and exact canonical entrypoint; retain the historical explanatory text in a new compatibility source.

Use SHA-pinned actions consistent with the trusted generated verifier and explicit workflow/ref concurrency. Preserve required checks on all relevant events, superseding-run cancellation, protected-tag exact-tree attestation and fail-closed behavior. Generated verifier changes only through the official generator. Composite abstractions require demonstrated benefit.

Historical compatibility is a supplementary versioned, source-hash-bound forward map. It may locate authentic existing closure records and distinguish missing source from unsupported shape. It cannot supply missing dates, signatures, records, retroactive authority or a passing native verdict. Reject changed source bytes, identity mismatch, duplicates and unregistered new exceptions. Preserve both chains and all sealed bytes; an actual historical mutation remains a finding.

Threat and error documents normalize existing canonical controls and source-backed negative examples; draft domain decisions retain their status. Product mitigation, privacy compliance and production readiness require their own evidence.

## Validation

Run law/schema/trace, official binding and doctor checks, native harness sensors on the frozen tree, execution-backed invariant producers, concurrency/isolation checks, and negative compatibility tampering tests. Confirm exact sealed-byte preservation and unchanged thresholds. Review cross-family before PR; integrate only with all required checks green. This decision admits no blanket legacy exemption and does not replace provider validators.
