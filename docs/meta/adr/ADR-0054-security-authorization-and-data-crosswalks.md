# ADR-0054: Source-bound authorization and personal-data crosswalks

## Status

Accepted by Architect on2026-10-19 under the Owner-authorized C-0004 scope.

## Context

The Owner authorized campaign C-0004 on2026-10-19. C3/C4 are representation and measurement changes; no permission, DDL, RLS, retention execution or application behavior changes.

## Decision

Use deterministic source-bound crosswalks under law/security with project-owned closed schemas under docs/framework/schemas. Authorization preserves Resource/Action, contextual predicates and public/mock/fixture dispositions; it never creates a grant from a role-name guess. Every known endpoint remains in the census. Public endpoints carry compensating controls and real test references. Exporters reject omitted, duplicate or stale source coordinates.

PII combines native SQL candidates and explicit blueprint classifications, retaining union provenance rather than counting the heuristic as complete. Each field names classification, purpose, legal basis and retention sources or explicit pending decisions. Unvalidated drafts remain drafts. Owner delegation permits engineering choices; it does not establish legal facts, institutional authority or statutory deadlines.

Installed DEVAI2.6.3 does not consume these project formats. Native readings and supplemental review remain separate; no native unresolved count or compliance verdict is manually replaced. A provider extension requires its own bounded proposal, implementation, tests and authorized release.

## Validation and limits

Validate strict schemas, deterministic export and negative provenance tests; rerun actual policy/decorator/role checks. Freeze candidate before native inventory generation. The round report must identify source producer commit, evaluated commit, hashes and unresolved runtime/legal evidence. The canonical record is R-0039. R-0036 and R-0037 remain immutable; thresholds and proof histories remain unchanged.
