# ADR-0060: Consolidate the completion roadmap without replacing acceptance

## Status

Accepted by direct Owner approval on2026-10-23. Recording/integration is proposed
through this PR. This supplements ADR-0042; it changes scheduling and coordination,
not original product acceptance, substrate authority, scope or historical standing.

## Context

The Owner consolidated the Backlog and Scorecard chats, approved the resulting
eight-milestone roadmap and requested a new completion-plan PR followed by beginning
M1. Product delivery, scorecard measurement and provider recovery had distinct
source candidates and authority gates. Their consolidation must not merge verdicts
or erase incomplete original-round obligations.

## Decision

Use [COMPLETION-PLAN.md §11](../../../COMPLETION-PLAN.md#11-approved-consolidated-roadmap--2026-10-23)
as the single remaining-work schedule. Retain CP-00…CP-11 acceptance and the40JNY/
110UC/12overlappingJW crosswalk, all64RAIT commands plus approved additions,
round/task identity, decisions, exclusions and D/Q/O milestones. Milestone labels
are planning references, not new DEVAI rounds or lifecycle states.

One coordinating chat may plan distinct governed workstreams and role sessions.
M1 begins with current-main attestation and supported provider-adoption/measurement
work. M2 recovery authority, #211 merge, S3 runtime and C6 activation remain held
at their original gates. Portal/Dashboard may accompany the producers they consume
after the first complete analyst journey; shared locks and actual dependencies
remain. PEC retains its prerequisite/final placement; necessary platform slices
remain bounded under approved P2. Help, truthful availability, accessibility,
security and negative tests accompany every delivery.

## Preservation and authority

Retain original campaigns, ADRs, histories, proof chains, readings, closed rounds,
local files, staged reconciliation and recoverable refs. No source/test/runtime
change or generated output is authorized by this ADR. Dependency and registered
migration execution belongs to separately bounded role sessions; no upstream write,
release, legal fact, external contact or field/deployment authority is inferred.
C6's whole-nine deferral and refusal handling remain unchanged under ADR-0057.
M1 may finish an assessment with explicit failures/blockers; it cannot declare C6
or an incomplete campaign complete. Required-green PR integration remains binding.

## Validation

Verify unchanged §3/§4/§9 completion/acceptance sections and historical ADR/proof/
closed-round bytes against main80505ac0. Run formatting, KB/publication, state/round
indexes and doctor; independent review before PR. Report M1 execution separately
from this documentation-only change, with exact source SHA and actual receipts.
