# ADR-0014: WF-INF-003 is the canonical infraction lifecycle state machine

## Status

Accepted on 2026-09-12 by Owner decision. Supersedes the master lifecycle
`WF-INF-001` in the business knowledge base; does not alter ADR-0001…0011.

## Context

`docs/framework/product/shared/workflows/WF-INF-001.md` was the draft master
lifecycle of a traffic infraction that cross-cuts teat, rait, portal and
dashboard. It predated the legal round that produced `RN-RAIT-101…138` and the
BPO rounds that produced `WF-RAIT-001…003` and `WF-TEAT-001`, and it left the
consequence of its 180/360-day timers "to confirm", modelled payment as a
terminal state, carried no state for the 30-day second-instance window and no
terminal for prescription by inertia (CTB art. 289-A) or decay (CTB art. 282 §7º).

A BPM modelling round on 2026-09-12 produced two artifacts: `WF-INF-002`, the
BPMN model of every infraction-related process (lavratura, notification,
driver indication, prior defense, JARI appeal, collegiate session, CETRAN appeal
and closure, public portal) with a unified catalogue of automatic timers; and
`WF-INF-003`, the state machine derived from it. The Owner read both and decided
that `WF-INF-003` replaces `WF-INF-001`.

## Decision

1. `WF-INF-003` is the canonical state vocabulary of the infraction lifecycle.
   It is promoted to `reviewed`. `WF-INF-002` remains the derivation source
   (`draft` until its own review) and owns the unified timer catalogue.
2. `WF-INF-001` is kept as a pointer artifact (`superseded_by: WF-INF-003`) so
   historical bracket references keep resolving; it no longer defines states,
   transitions or deadlines. Its previous content lives in Git history.
3. Consumers are rewritten against the new vocabulary: the INF domain charter,
   `WF-RAIT-001` §Ponte (case ↔ infraction mapping and event consumers),
   `WF-TEAT-001` §Ponte (integration hand-off and the two adopted encaixes),
   `WF-RAIT-002`, `RN-RAIT-108`, TEAT/BOAT/PORTAL rules and use cases, the
   shared actor catalogue, the live legal references and the knowledge-base
   index. `_intake/` research notes and the raw CTB capture are provenance and
   are left untouched.
4. Timer identifiers already used by generated code and existing workflows
   (`T-REM10`, `T-DIL`, `T-R2`, `T-JUL-24M`, `T-PAR-3A`, `T-DEC`, `T-VOTO`,
   `T-CONV`, `T-SNE-CIENCIA`) are preserved with the same meaning; the new
   infraction-level timers are `T-NA`, `T-DEF`, `T-IND`, `T-NA-IND`,
   `T-NP-VENC` and `T-PRESC-5A`.
5. Substantive changes accepted with the new machine: decay (CTB art. 282 §7º)
   and prescription (CTB art. 289-A; Lei 9.873/1999) become their own terminal
   states; the 30-day post-JARI window becomes a state with two legitimated
   parties; payment no longer closes the instance except with acknowledgement
   (CTB art. 290 III); archival gains the `insubsistente` motive (CTB art. 281
   §1º I); withdrawal does not close the instance by itself (`RN-RAIT-123`);
   there is no post-closure revision channel (Owner steering C.22).

## Consequences

- **No runtime code references `WF-INF-001`.** The backend modules under
  `backend/domains/inf/*` are generated from `docs/framework/blueprints/*.json`
  (ADR-0007) and those blueprints cite only `WF-RAIT-001…003`; the AIT module
  implements the talão lifecycle of `WF-TEAT-001`. Nothing is regenerated by
  this ADR.
- **The infraction aggregate is not implemented yet.** Implementing
  `WF-INF-003` requires a new blueprint (working id `BP-INF-INFRACTION-001`,
  namespace `inf`, depending on `@detran/inf-ait` and `@detran/inf-rait-case`)
  declaring the infraction entity with `state`, `sujeito_passivo`, the
  per-notification `ciencia` records (channel, expedition, effective/ficta
  date, printed deadline), `efeito_suspensivo`, payment attributes and
  closure motive; an infraction timer table constrained to the §9.2 catalogue
  of `WF-INF-002`; the transition table of `WF-INF-003` §2 as command rules;
  and the domain events of `WF-INF-003` §6. That is Phase 3 engineering work
  under a separate change, after Architect review of the module boundary.
- **National mirror.** `WF-INF-003` §8 maps each state to the mock
  `SituacaoRenainf` enumeration exposed by the senatran-adapter contract; the
  mapping must be validated against the real RENAINF contract before the
  artifact is `approved`.
- **Corpus tooling.** `pnpm docs:kb:check` baselines in
  `docs/meta/knowledge-base/import-manifest.json` are updated for the new
  artifacts and canonical tokens; publication stays fail-closed (only
  `reviewed`/`approved` product artifacts are published).
- Open modelling questions inherited from the round remain listed in
  `WF-INF-002` and `WF-INF-003` "Decisões de modelagem pendentes" and in the
  knowledge-base backlog.
